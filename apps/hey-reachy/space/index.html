What it does

A conversation, not a command line

Most robot apps treat the robot as a button you press. This one you talk to, and it talks back like it's sitting there with you.

Keyless wake word

An open-source wake word (openWakeWord) wakes the robot. No cloud, no account, no API key to start listening.

Pluggable brain

Conversation runs through one interface. Point it at any OpenAI-compatible model, or wire in your own agent. Swap the engine anytime.

Speaks from the robot

Replies come out of Reachy's own speaker in a natural voice, and it keeps listening for a few seconds so you can go back and forth.

Calm by design

A small, slow head-nod while it speaks, antennas pinned, a level pose the rest of the time. Present, not fidgety.

The signature is the listening pulse: the waveform breathes cyan while Reachy speaks, and your turn is always the warm one. You can see who has the floor.
